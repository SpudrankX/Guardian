Guardian OS

Overview

Guardian is the knowledge and research operating system behind the TRYX ecosystem.

Its purpose is to collect, organize, validate, connect, and preserve market knowledge while supporting continuous learning and system development.

Guardian is not a trading strategy.

Guardian is not an automated trading bot.

Guardian is a research operating system designed to help the Researcher observe the markets objectively, build validated knowledge, and improve decision-making over time.

---

Mission

Transform raw market observations into validated knowledge.

Guardian exists to ensure that every lesson, observation, screenshot, journal entry, and research finding contributes to a growing knowledge base instead of being forgotten.

---

Core Principles

- Knowledge before automation.
- Research before conclusions.
- Validation before promotion.
- Documentation before memory.
- Consistency before complexity.

Guardian Responsibilities

Guardian is responsible for:

- Organizing knowledge
- Maintaining documentation
- Connecting related concepts
- Supporting research
- Explaining documented concepts
- Preserving version history
- Maintaining consistency across the TRYX ecosystem

Guardian is never responsible for making trading decisions.

The final authority always belongs to the Researcher.

Guardian will evolve from a documented research system into an intelligent operating system capable of:
